# Interview readiness

Three short labs that turn what you built into things you can say in a room. They show up in the build block after the D labs, and nothing here is theory: every story, bullet and talk points at a file in this repo.

| Lab | What | Steps |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Five STAR stories drawn from your own labs, three of them rehearsed out loud with Claude | 3 |
| E2 | Resume bullets generated from your actual progress by `coach.py resume`, then edited by you | 2 |
| E3 | Two ten-minute design talks, on the MCP server (C3) and the RAG system (C5), rehearsed with follow-ups | 2 |

Claude's side of these is in `COACHING.md` under "Interview readiness". Short version: Claude asks the questions, times you, and grades. Claude never writes your answers.
