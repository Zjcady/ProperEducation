# E1 rubric

| # | Criterion |
|---|---|
| 1 | Three stories are complete; each Action names at least one concrete artifact in this repo. |
| 2 | Every Result has a number or a specific observable outcome. |
| 3 | Each story is tied to a posting line and the learner can say why. |
| 4 | Spoken versions are under two minutes; the log shows times. |
| 5 | The learner can tell Story 5 without notes. |
| 6 | At least one story includes something that went wrong and what changed because of it. |
