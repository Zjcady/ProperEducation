# E1 · STAR stories from your own labs

**Maps to:** every behavioral question in the interview. Each story also points at a line in the posting.
**You'll have at the end:** five written stories in `STORIES.md`, three of them rehearsed and graded.

STAR is Situation, Task, Action, Result. Two minutes spoken. The Action is the part they're hiring; make it the longest.

## Step 1 · Pick  (15 min)

Open `STORIES.md`. Five slots, each tied to a posting line and a suggested source in this repo. For each slot write only the first two headings (Situation, Task) in two sentences each. Pick the three slots that feel strongest and mark them `rehearse: yes`. Then `python3 coach.py check E1`.

## Step 2 · Write  (15 min)

Fill in Action and Result for the three you marked. Action: what *you* did, in order, with one concrete detail an interviewer could ask about (a function name, a number, a decision you reversed). Result: what happened, with a number where one exists, and one sentence on what you'd do differently. Then `check`.

## Step 3 · Rehearse  (15 min)

Open a session and say "rehearse E1". Claude asks one story at a time as an interviewer would ("tell me about a time..."), times you, and grades specificity, structure and length. Add a row to the rehearsal log in `STORIES.md` for each one. Three rows pass the step. Then rubric review and `python3 coach.py done E1`.
