# E2 rubric

| # | Criterion |
|---|---|
| 1 | Every number in RESUME.md matches `coach.py resume` output or the learner can explain the difference. |
| 2 | Every bullet starts with a verb and has one number or one named artifact. |
| 3 | No bullet claims something the learner couldn't talk about for two minutes. |
| 4 | The headline says what the learner is, in one line, without the word "passionate". |
| 5 | Bullets fit on one line each. |
| 6 | The learner updated at least one template in bullets.json with their own wording. |
