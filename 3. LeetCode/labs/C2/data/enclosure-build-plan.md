# Bioactive enclosure build plan

Goal: 18x18x24 glass enclosure for the crested gecko, bioactive. Layers: drainage (LECA), mesh, 3 inches of ABG-style substrate, leaf litter. Plants: pothos, bromeliad, ficus. Cleanup crew: springtails and isopods, two weeks before the gecko moves in. Budget about $220. Lighting: low UVB 5.0, 12 hours a day on a timer.
